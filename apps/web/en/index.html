01 · The misunderstanding

You don't live at the same rhythm.

Tuesday, 8 pm. She comes home drained. He's found the holiday tickets and wants to talk about it right now. It goes badly, and neither of them understands why.

She's at the end of her cycle. He didn't know. Nobody's wrong: a piece of information was missing.

It's not a lack of goodwill. It's a gap in education. Phases fills it, without lecturing.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no in-app purchase, no ads. Phases has no servers to pay for, so no reason to charge you.

Does she need the app?

No. One date gets you started. If she has it, you can sync by QR code or link.

What if her cycle is irregular?

Phases learns from every confirmed cycle and shows a window instead of a date when cycles vary a lot.

Can I use it for my own cycle?

Yes: “it's my cycle” mode keeps the same reminders, written to you.

Where is my data? What if I switch iPhones?

On your iPhone, and in its iCloud backup if that's on. You can also export a backup file from Settings.

Android?

Not for now. Phases is built and tested for iPhone.
