A word from the maker

Why I built Phases.

So more couples get in sync. And because I believe this kind of thing is education.

Hi, I'm Guillaume.

A lot of arguments in a couple are badly timed. Not because people love each other badly, but because they don't live at the same rhythm, and they don't know it.

The menstrual cycle is part of that. It shifts energy, mood and needs from one week to the next. And most men know almost nothing about it. Nobody ever really explained it to us.

I built Phases to fill that gap. One date is enough, and the app tells you the day before what's coming, with what helps. You understand more, you anticipate, and you end up in sync more often.

I believe this is a matter of education. And education shouldn't be sold as a subscription. So Phases is free. No premium tier, no ads.

It's also some of the most intimate data there is. So Phases doesn't take it: everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no server. I see nothing, on purpose.

If the app helps you, tell people about it. That's the only marketing Phases will ever have.

Guillaume
