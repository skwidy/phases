Privacy

Last updated: September 27, 2026.

In one sentence

Phases collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

The app

  • Cycle dates, settings and reminders are stored on the device only.
  • The app makes no network requests: no server, no account, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs.
  • Data is included in your iPhone's iCloud backup if you turned it on; that backup is handled by Apple, not Phases.
  • "Erase everything" in Settings deletes all data and cancels every reminder.

Syncing between two phones

The QR code or share link holds cycle start dates and lengths, nothing else. In the link, that data sits after the "#" sign: browsers never send it to the server, ours included. The app carrying the link (iMessage, WhatsApp…) follows its own policy.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no fonts or scripts loaded from third parties. The host (Vercel) may keep standard technical logs (IP address, date) for security.
